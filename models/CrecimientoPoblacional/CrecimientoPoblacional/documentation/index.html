CrecimientoPoblacional — Logistic Population Growth

Classic logistic equation with carrying capacity K=100 and growth rate r=3.5.

Starting from N=2, the population grows through the inflection point (N=50) and converges to K=100.

Integration challenge

Near N=K/2=50, the growth rate is maximum (87.5/time). Euler overshoots K and oscillates; RK4 converges monotonically.

Use tasa_entrada=0 for the pure logistic test case.
