================================================================================
  TEST BATTERY — OsciladorArmonico
  Undamped Harmonic Oscillator — Integration Quality / Energy Conservation
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
OsciladorArmonico is a purely undamped harmonic oscillator. It has no friction,
no energy dissipation, and no external forcing. In the exact (analytical)
solution, the total mechanical energy is perfectly constant for all time.

This property makes it an ideal benchmark for the NUMERICAL INTEGRATOR used
by the codegen tool. Any integrator that is not symplectic (energy-preserving)
will either gain or lose energy over time — and this drift is directly
observable in the 'energia' output.

WHY THIS MODEL EXISTS
---------------------
The codegen tool generates a main.c that includes a numerical ODE solver.
This model verifies:
  1. Whether the solver conserves energy (RK4 does well; Euler does not)
  2. Whether the solver can run for many oscillation cycles without diverging
  3. Whether the 'posicion' output matches the known analytical solution

PHYSICS
-------
ODE: x'' + ω²·x = 0
Split: dx/dt = v,   dv/dt = -ω²·x
Initial conditions: x(0) = 1.0 m,  v(0) = 0.0 m/s
Parameter: omega = 2π rad/s (one cycle per second)

Exact solution:
  x(t)   = cos(ω·t)
  v(t)   = -ω·sin(ω·t)
  E(t)   = 0.5·v² + 0.5·ω²·x² = 0.5·ω² ≈ 19.739 J  (constant)

Key quantities:
  Period: T = 2π/ω = 1.0 s
  Initial energy: E₀ = 0.5·(2π)² ≈ 19.739 J

FMU INTERFACE
-------------
  Inputs:   NONE

  Outputs:
    posicion  (Real) — displacement x(t) [m],  exact: cos(2π·t)
    velocidad (Real) — velocity v(t) [m/s],    exact: -2π·sin(2π·t)
    energia   (Real) — total energy [J],       exact: 19.739 (constant)

EXPORT INSTRUCTIONS
-------------------
  1. checkModel(OsciladorArmonico) — expect 2 states, 0 inputs
  2. buildModelFMU(OsciladorArmonico, version="2.0", fmuType="me")
  3. Run codegen on the FMU — generate main.c with both Euler and RK4
  4. Simulate both and compare energia drift over 50 seconds

================================================================================
  TEST CASES
================================================================================

TEST-OA-001 — Initial conditions check (t = 0)
------------------------------------------------
  Expected:
    posicion  = 1.0          (= x0 = cos(0))
    velocidad = 0.0          (= v0 = -2π·sin(0))
    energia   ≈ 19.739       (= 0.5·(2π)²)
  Tolerance: ±1e-6
  Rationale: Verifies initialization is correct before any integration step.

TEST-OA-002 — Analytical match at t = 0.25 s (quarter period)
---------------------------------------------------------------
  At t = 0.25 s = T/4:
    x(0.25)  = cos(2π·0.25) = cos(π/2) = 0.0
    v(0.25)  = -2π·sin(π/2) = -2π ≈ -6.283
    E(0.25)  ≈ 19.739 (unchanged)
  Expected:
    posicion  ≈  0.0    (tolerance ±0.01)
    velocidad ≈ -6.283  (tolerance ±0.05)
    energia   ≈ 19.739  (tolerance ±0.1)
  Rationale: Cross-check against analytical solution at a known point.

TEST-OA-003 — Analytical match at t = 0.5 s (half period)
-----------------------------------------------------------
  At t = 0.5 s = T/2:
    x(0.5)  = cos(π) = -1.0
    v(0.5)  = -2π·sin(π) = 0.0
    E(0.5)  ≈ 19.739
  Expected:
    posicion  ≈ -1.0   (tolerance ±0.01)
    velocidad ≈  0.0   (tolerance ±0.05)
    energia   ≈ 19.739 (tolerance ±0.1)
  Rationale: Position returns to -amplitude at half period.

TEST-OA-004 — Analytical match at t = 1.0 s (full period)
-----------------------------------------------------------
  At t = 1.0 s = T:
    x(1.0) = cos(2π) = 1.0
    v(1.0) = -2π·sin(2π) = 0.0
  Expected:
    posicion  ≈ 1.0    (tolerance ±0.02)
    velocidad ≈ 0.0    (tolerance ±0.1)
    energia   ≈ 19.739 (tolerance ±0.2)
  Rationale: System must return to initial state after exactly one period.

TEST-OA-005 — Energy conservation over 20 cycles
--------------------------------------------------
  Simulate: t_end = 20.0 s, h = 0.01 s (step = T/100)
  At every output point, compute energy drift: ΔE = energia - 19.739
  Expected with RK4:
    max |ΔE| < 0.05 J  (< 0.25% relative error)
  Expected with Euler:
    energia at t=20 > 20.5 J  (Euler adds energy — drift > 3%)
  Rationale: PRIMARY TEST. This is the observable difference between
             integrators. RK4 passes; Euler fails.

TEST-OA-006 — Energy drift over 100 cycles
--------------------------------------------
  Simulate: t_end = 100.0 s, h = 0.01 s
  Expected with RK4:
    max |ΔE| < 0.5 J   (< 2.5% relative error over 100 cycles)
  Expected with Euler:
    energia at t=100 >> 30 J  (catastrophic drift)
  Rationale: Long-term stability check. RK4 degrades slowly; Euler diverges.

TEST-OA-007 — Position stays bounded
--------------------------------------
  Simulate: t_end = 50.0 s, h = 0.01 s
  Expected with RK4:
    |posicion| ≤ 1.5 at all times  (slight growth due to RK4 error is OK)
  Expected with Euler:
    |posicion| > 3.0 at some point before t=50 (unbounded amplitude growth)
  Rationale: Amplitude growth directly visible in the position output.

TEST-OA-008 — Velocity extrema match theory
---------------------------------------------
  At every zero-crossing of posicion, |velocidad| should be ≈ ω = 2π ≈ 6.283
  Check: for all t where |posicion| < 0.05, verify |velocidad| ≈ 6.283 ± 0.3
  Rationale: Kinetic energy is maximum at x=0 — energy is fully kinetic.

TEST-OA-009 — Small step size comparison
------------------------------------------
  Run two simulations with RK4:
    Sim A: h = 0.01 s
    Sim B: h = 0.001 s
  At t = 10.0 s, compare energia:
    |energia_A - energia_B| < 0.001 J
  Rationale: Verifies RK4 convergence — smaller step produces better result.

TEST-OA-010 — Codegen structural check
----------------------------------------
  After codegen with RK4, inspect generated C source for:
    [ ] 4 derivative evaluations per step (k1, k2, k3, k4 in RK4)
    [ ] State vector of size 2 (x and v)
    [ ] No fmi2Set* calls in step loop (zero inputs)
    [ ] fmi2GetReal called 3 times after each step
    [ ] RK4_BUF_SIZE or equivalent constant ≥ 2
  Rationale: Structural audit that RK4 was correctly generated for this FMU.

================================================================================
  TOLERANCE SUMMARY

  Initial conditions:       absolute tolerance = 1e-6
  Analytical cross-checks:  position tolerance = ±0.02 m
                            velocity tolerance = ±0.1 m/s
                            energy tolerance   = ±0.2 J
  Energy conservation (RK4): max drift < 0.05 J over 20 cycles
  Energy drift (Euler):       must drift > 0.8 J over 20 cycles (to confirm
