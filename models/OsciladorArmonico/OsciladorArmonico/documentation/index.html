OsciladorArmonico — Undamped Harmonic Oscillator

Classic undamped oscillator. The total mechanical energy must be conserved for all time (no damping).

This model is an integration quality benchmark: Euler adds energy artificially (amplitude grows), while RK4 conserves it accurately.

Exact solution

x(t) = cos(ω·t), E(t) = 0.5·ω² ≈ 19.739 J (constant)

Key observable

Plot energia over 50+ seconds. With Euler (h=0.01): energy drifts upward. With RK4 (h=0.01): energy stays flat.
