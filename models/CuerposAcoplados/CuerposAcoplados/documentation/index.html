CuerposAcoplados — Two Coupled Masses

Two masses connected by wall springs and a coupling spring. With non-zero initial conditions and no external force, the system exhibits energy exchange between bodies (beating).

Key properties

  • 4 continuous states — verifies codegen buffer size scaling
  • Energy conservation — total energy ≈ 3.625 J (when F=0)
  • Boolean output — en_fase transitions as bodies go in/out of phase

With Euler (h=0.05) and fuerza_externa=0: energia_total drifts upward. With RK4 (h=0.05): energia_total stays within 0.01 J of 3.625 J.
