PenduloNoLineal — Large-Angle Nonlinear Pendulum

Undamped pendulum released from 150° (2.618 rad). The nonlinear term sin(θ) dominates at this angle, making accurate integration essential.

Energy conservation

E₀ = -g·L·cos(150°) ≈ 8.496 J must remain constant.

With Euler (h=0.05): energy error exceeds 5% before t=10 s.

With RK4 (h=0.05): energy stays within 0.1% for 50+ seconds.
