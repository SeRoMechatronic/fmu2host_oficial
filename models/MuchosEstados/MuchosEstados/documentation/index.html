MuchosEstados — 8 Cascaded First-Order Filters

Eight first-order filters in series, each with time constant τ=1.0 s. The cascade has 8 continuous states and is the primary test for ODE buffer size scaling in the codegen tool.

Buffer size requirement

The codegen must allocate state vectors of at least size 8 for both Euler and RK4 buffers. If EULER_BUF_SIZE or RK4_BUF_SIZE is hardcoded below 8, this model produces incorrect results or a crash.

Step response

With u=1.0 step, y8 reaches its peak near t=8·τ=8 s at approximately y8_peak ≈ 0.200 (8th-order cascade step response peak).
